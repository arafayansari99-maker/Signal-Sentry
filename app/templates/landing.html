What this app does

New user guide

SignalSentry helps you stay ahead of the market by automatically tracking competitor pages, spotting pricing and product changes, and turning those shifts into short, actionable insights. If you are new here, follow the steps below to understand how the platform works from start to finish.

1

Add a competitor

Start by adding a company name and domain. The app stores the competitor and begins the monitoring setup.

2

Track key pages

Attach the pages you care about most, such as pricing, product, careers, or messaging pages.

3

Monitor and compare

The system checks those pages over time, captures content snapshots, and highlights meaningful changes.

4

See alerts and digest

High-impact changes are grouped into digest insights and alerts so your team can act quickly.
